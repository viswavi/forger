# Task: Cultural Commonsense Reasoning

You must generate a training dataset to help models like yourself improve at the task of cultural commonsense reasoning. In this setting, the task is to classify whether a given scenario depicts characters acting in ways aligned with known cultural norms in that country ("Yes"), violating a social norm in that country ("No"), or neither adhering to nor violating any significant social norms in that country ("Neutral"). Models are currently fairly good at identifying "Yes" or "No" cases; our goal is to maintain or improve models' precision in this setting while significantly improving our ability to identify "Neutral" cases.

Public validation examples are shown in {TASK_DIR}/validation_tasks_de_obfuscated.jsonl -- use these however you like. Each contains the following keys: ["id", "country",  "situation", "gold_label", "task_text", "axis", "subaxis", "value", "rule_of_thumb"]. If compatible baseline outputs are available, you will also find model output files {TASK_DIR}/model_outputs/Qwen__Qwen3-8B_tinker/val/metrics.json (summary metrics) and {TASK_DIR}/model_outputs/Qwen__Qwen3-8B_tinker/val/predictions.jsonl (per-row predictions on the validation set). Feel free to use these predictions/summary metrics to inform your data generation approach.

While the validation rows contain many keys, the ones you actually need are "country", "situation", and "gold_label" (as the source of the target answer). You may generate others as support if helpful, but they will be ignored during training. Concretely, our output data only need to have the following keys:
{{"country": ..., "situation": ..., "question": ..., "thinking": ..., "answer": ...}}
where "answer" is the plain-text label ("yes"/"no"/"neutral", matching "gold_label" from the validation rows) -- NOT the key name "gold_label" itself.

Note that this the output described here must include long-form "thinking" traces that are well-suited to the student model we are finetuning ({STUDENT_MODEL}) to help us train on this data in "thinking" mode. You should generate no more than 2000 generated datapoints -- below that, you can choose which examples to generate to maximize the accuracy of the model. Max sequence length for training is {MAX_SFT_LENGTH} tokens -- longer rows are dropped before training, so avoid generating overly-long thinking traces when possible.
